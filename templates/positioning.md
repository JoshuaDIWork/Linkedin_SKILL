## Who we are writing for

- IT leaders, Heads of Digital, CTOs and CIOs at mid-to-large Australian
  organisations and government agencies.
- Atlassian administrators and platform owners.
- Operations and transformation leads working out what to do about AI.
- Decision-makers evaluating an Atlassian partner.

They care about getting more from the Atlassian spend they already have,
reducing waste, AI that does a real job rather than a demo, and something they
can apply on Monday. They are at a desk in AEST/AEDT.

The 2026 LinkedIn Ads ran into an audience problem: most clicks came from
engineers and junior staff, and CIOs saw the ads without clicking. Write to
the buyer, not to whoever clicks most.

## Banned in DI copy

The humaniser flags every one of these.

- Gold, Platinum, and Enterprise Partner(s), in any partner wording.
- "DI AI Foundation".
- Discount or "Save $" language, prices, "was $", "down from $".
- Hours as the lead of an offer, and any Foundation Package figure.
- IRAP, ISO 27001 or SOC 2 framed as a DI service or certification.
- A Community of Practice described as training.
- Unsourced trust or scale claims: "trusted by enterprises nationwide",
  "Australia's leading", "500 enterprises" with no source.
- A "2-3 weeks" that does not name the Claude or Rovo track.
- A client name not on the approved list.
- Em dashes, American spelling, miscased Atlassian product names.

## House rules

- Australian English, always: organisation, optimise, licence (noun), centre,
  programme, colour. The humaniser fixes what slips through.
- **Never an em dash.** Comma, colon, full stop.
- One to two emoji per post at most. None as a first character.
- Never criticise a competitor or another partner by name.
- No political commentary.
- No confidential client information. Assume every draft is public.
- Atlassian product names are spelt Atlassian's way: Jira, Jira Service
  Management, Confluence, Rovo, Rovo Dev, Atlassian Intelligence, Data
  Center, Bitbucket, Opsgenie, Statuspage, Trello, Loom, Compass, Jira
  Product Discovery.
- **Source content is DI-owned.** Repurpose DI's own articles, webinars and
  case studies. Never republish Atlassian's own content under the DI name.
- Post Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9:00am AEST, two a week minimum. Avoid
  weekends and public holidays, including Anzac Day, Easter, Melbourne Cup
  and Christmas to New Year. Reply to comments inside two hours.
- Images follow the VISS Framework (see the Image Creation Register).
- Every link to di.net.au carries UTM tags: `utm_source=linkedin` with
  `utm_medium=organic` for organic posts and `utm_medium=paid` for ads, so
  GA4 can add LinkedIn up.
- Paid spend is reported in USD, following the Platform Standards Reference
  for Paid Media (ARL), so figures reconcile with Cascade.
