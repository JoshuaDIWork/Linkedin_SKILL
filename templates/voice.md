# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/di-linkedin/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in
the pack reads it, alongside `positioning.md`, which carries the company facts
so this file only has to carry the person.

There is one of these per person who posts. Michael's is the default. A team
member setting up their own profile copies the template, fills it in, and
points the skills at it.

If you would rather not fill it in by hand, paste three of your own posts into
Claude and say "write my voice.md from these". That works better than guessing
at the answers.
